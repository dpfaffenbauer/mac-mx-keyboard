## 2. What the Software does on your computer

- **Lock screen** locks your Mac through a macOS system function, or, where that function is not available,
  by sending the keyboard shortcut Control-Command-Q through System Events. macOS asks for your permission
  before the shortcut works.
- **Amphetamine keys** read the state of the app Amphetamine through its AppleScript interface while it runs,
  and, when you press a key, end the current Amphetamine session and start a new one with the remaining time
  plus the key's time, or launch Amphetamine. macOS asks for your permission before this works.

The Software does not write any files outside its own plugin folder and does not change settings of other apps
beyond the Amphetamine session you extend.
